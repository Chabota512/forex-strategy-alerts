1. PURPOSE

Build a small, local, file-based analysis tool that reads OHLC candle CSVs,
applies the owner-provided candle-trigger specification, writes an informational
alert log, and reports reward scores and validation floors. It is a research and
analysis tool, not a trading system.

The build will use Python's standard library, CSV, and JSON files. It will not
add a web service, database, authentication, or background process.


2. HARD CONSTRAINTS

- Alerts are informational. The owner decides whether to trade.
- No order placement, automatic trading, take-profit, or stop-loss behavior.
- No claims about profitability or probability of success.
- No MT5 dependency.
- Synthetic prices must be clearly labeled and must never be presented as
  historical or live market data.
- The original seed specification must remain preserved and unedited after it
  is established.
- Do not promote a candidate automatically. Any later promotion requires the
  owner's explicit approval.
- Do not silently change strategy rules, score floors, or thresholds to make a
  result pass.
- Every alert log and report must record the spec version and effective settings,
  including CST and reward settings. Include a stable settings fingerprint and
  input-file hashes so setting or data drift is visible between runs.
- When real Dukascopy data is supplied, compare a sample against a second free
  source and report discrepancies before trusting scores. No real-data scoring
  proceeds until the owner reviews and approves a comparison tied to the exact
  input-file hash.


3. SOURCE MATERIAL AND DECISIONS

Use the owner-provided files as the source of truth:

- candle_trigger_spec_v1.5: trigger, CCT, CST, and qualitative strength behavior.
- strategy_package_for_build: total-validation-reward objective, thresholds,
  floors, and the Phase 0/1 implementation scope.
- the owner's latest reply: complete reward outcome rules, 12-candle lookahead,
  train/validation split, and CST settings.

Confirmed trigger:

- Evaluate closed candles only, comparing each current candle to its immediate
  predecessor.
- SELL requires current.high > previous.high, current.close < current.open, and
  previous.close > previous.open.
- BUY requires current.low < previous.low, current.close > current.open, and
  previous.close < previous.open.
- Wick comparisons are strict. A touch is not a break.
- A perfect current-candle doji does not alert. A previous doji does not satisfy
  either strict color condition.
- The close direction determines the alert when the candle breaks both
  predecessor extremes.
- CCT is descriptive only and may not create, suppress, or alter an alert.
- CST determines only the strength label. WEAK alerts still fire.

Confirmed reward settings:

- D_min/A_max are price-unit distances:
    GBPUSD: 0.0010 / 0.0008
    USDJPY: 0.12 / 0.10
    XAUUSD: 0.30 / 0.25
- Score each alert using exactly the next 12 closed candles in its own
  timeframe. Do not use the alert candle's range or candles beyond that window.
- If no +D_min target is reached in the window, score -1.
- If +D_min is reached but the adverse threshold was reached first, score +0.5.
- If +D_min is reached before the adverse threshold, score +1, or +1.5 when the
  target is reached within the first 6 candles.
- Add +0.1 for each additional full D_min of favorable follow-through, capped
  at +0.5.
- The optimization objective is total validation-period reward, not average
  reward per alert.
- Use the last 12 consecutive calendar months in the data: months 1-9 are
  training, months 10-12 are validation.
- Validation floors: at least 100 validation alerts per symbol/timeframe pair;
  alerts in at least 8 of the 12 months overall.
- Do not relax a floor if it fails. Report the failure to the owner.
- Any future challenger must beat the champion's validation score by at least
  10%, and promotion remains manual.

Confirmed CST settings:

- Major swings use a strict fractal extreme with 5 candles on each side.
- CCT minor swings use 2 candles on each side, in the last 15 closed candles.
- CST uses uptrend, downtrend, undefined, and transition states.
- The owner-locked structure-break basis is candle close; this is a decision, not
  an open assumption. Do not change it during the current implementation.
- Strength is STRONG when alert direction aligns with CST, WEAK when it opposes
  CST, and NEUTRAL for undefined or transition CST.
- Strength labels do not suppress alerts.

Confirmed data-source change:

- OANDA is dropped because it is region-blocked.
- Phase 0 reads local CSV files only. It must not call or depend on any API.
- Keep the candle loader source-agnostic. Its public contract is
  load_candles(symbol, timeframe) -> list of {time, open, high, low, close}.
- Use canonical symbols without slashes and a consistent local filename pattern,
  such as lab/data/GBPUSD_M1.csv. The loader maps its symbol/timeframe inputs to
  local CSV paths, not to a vendor or network service.
- Return time as an ISO-8601 UTC string and OHLC values as validated decimals.
  The input CSV contract is time,open,high,low,close; vendor-specific parsing is
  not part of the loader.
- The owner expects to provide Dukascopy historical CSV exports for GBP/USD,
  USD/JPY, and XAU/USD, with M1 UTC bid candles. The loader must not require
  Dukascopy-specific network access or credentials.
- Continue using generated synthetic M5/M15 samples for the current backtest.
  After Phase 1 acceptance, add a standalone UTC M1-to-M5/M15 resampler for
  GBPUSD_M1.csv-style input.
- The future source sanity check is a separate local-file comparison. It does not
  add network calls to the loader or resample M1 data; comparison requires
  timestamp-aligned CSVs and reports differences without inventing a tolerance.


4. IMPLEMENTATION SEQUENCE

Step 1 — Freeze the owner-approved machine-readable seed

- Keep the original owner-provided Markdown specification intact.
- Preserve the approved trigger, CCT/CST settings, reward rules, thresholds,
  validation split, and floors as seed version 1.5.0.
- The owner has approved freezing 1.5.0; keep champion.json as its frozen copy.
- Do not add the Gemini proposal loop or promotion workflow in this phase.

Step 2 — Add a provider-neutral CSV candle loader

- Implement the requested public function:
  load_candles(symbol, timeframe) -> list of {time, open, high, low, close}.
- Read local CSV files only; do not make HTTP requests, use provider
  credentials, or branch on a data-vendor name.
- Normalize timestamps to UTC and OHLC values to validated decimal prices.
- Reject invalid OHLC ranges, duplicate or out-of-order timestamps, missing
  columns, and empty files with a clear error.
- Accept the owner's future M1 UTC bid CSVs using the standard OHLC column
  contract. Keep M1 as M1; do not silently resample it into another timeframe.

Step 3 — Generate repeatable synthetic OHLC data

- Generate separate CSV files for GBPUSD, USDJPY, and XAUUSD on M5 and M15.
- Generate 12 consecutive calendar-month buckets, ending on a supplied UTC
  end-date (defaulting to the current UTC date).
- Generate weekday candles only and use a fixed default seed so repeated runs
  are reproducible.
- Include the source label "synthetic" and print a prominent synthetic-data
  warning.
- Use synthetic M5/M15 files for the current Phase 1 backtest. Do not connect
  to OANDA or any other API.

Step 4 — Implement the core trigger

- Apply the strict v1.5 trigger once per closed candle.
- Preserve the optional smaller-body observation as a note only; it cannot
  create or block an alert. The owner-labeled fixtures lock the note to current
  candle bodies strictly less than half the prior body.

Step 5 — Implement CCT and CST context

- CCT: last 15 closed candles; strict local extremes with 2 candles on each
  side; classify uptrend only when the last two highs and lows both rise,
  downtrend only when both fall, otherwise ranging.
- Do not treat a swing as confirmed until its required candles on the right
  have closed. Do not use future candles to label an earlier alert.
- CST: strict local extremes with 5 candles on each side, confirmed only after
  those right-side candles close.
- Implement the uptrend/downtrend/undefined/transition state machine. A CHoCH
  moves the prior state into transition; a later structure break confirms the
  new direction, while a break beyond the saved prior-trend level resumes the
  old direction.
- Keep the close-versus-wick basis in one configuration setting, defaulting to
  close.
- Derive STRONG/NEUTRAL/WEAK from the alert direction and CST state only.
- CST must not change whether the base trigger emits an alert.

Step 6 — Implement per-alert reward scoring

- Use the alert candle's close as the alert reference price.
- Examine only the next 12 candles, starting with the candle immediately after
  the alert candle.
- For BUY, favorable movement is future high minus entry and adverse movement
  is entry minus future low. Mirror these calculations for SELL.
- Treat reaching D_min as inclusive; "beyond" A_max is strict.
- Apply the base score from the first target/adverse event, then the speed bonus
  and follow-through bonus specified above.
- If fewer than 12 future candles exist, mark the alert
  INSUFFICIENT_LOOKAHEAD. Do not invent a score for it.
- Preserve the score, score status, and evaluation period for every alert in the
  CSV log.

Step 7 — Implement chronological split, report, and CSV output

- Select the last 12 consecutive calendar months represented by the loaded data.
- Label the first 9 months training and the final 3 months validation.
- Report total validation reward only as the objective score.
- Also report validation alert counts and summed reward split by STRONG, NEUTRAL,
  and WEAK, for each symbol/timeframe and overall. Counts include
  INSUFFICIENT_LOOKAHEAD alerts; reward sums include only scored alerts.
- Explain in the report that reward is blind to CST strength labels, so the
  stratified statistics are descriptive evidence and do not alter eligibility.
- Report validation alert counts for each symbol/timeframe pair and whether
  each count floor passes.
- Report the union of months containing alerts across all pairs and whether the
  8-of-12 coverage floor passes.
- Count alerts toward alert floors even if the final 12-candle reward window is
  incomplete; separately report how many alerts could not be scored.
- If the data does not contain 12 consecutive months, do not claim a validation
  pass or score. Explain what data is missing.
- Write a gzipped CSV (.csv.gz) with time, symbol, timeframe, direction, CCT, CST, strength label,
  optional note, period, reward score, and reward status. Each alert row also
  records spec version/status, CST and reward settings, settings fingerprint,
  input source/hash, and a reproducible run ID.
- Write a JSON report with the full effective settings, spec version/status,
  configuration fingerprint, data file hashes, source provenance, and validation
  results. Print the same settings/provenance in the terminal report.
- Keep all generated and user-supplied market CSVs and outputs out of Git.

Step 8 — Verify acceptance criteria

- Verify the eight labels listed in the strategy specification with
  representative synthetic OHLC pairs: four SELL, two BUY, and two NO ALERT.
  The original chart images and exact chart OHLC values were not supplied, so
  these tests confirm the written labels and rule behavior, not pixel-level
  reproduction of the source charts.
- Test strict equal-high/equal-low behavior, dojis, both-extremes-broken
  candles, and invalid OHLC input.
- Test score boundaries: no target, target before adverse, adverse before
  target, speed bonus boundary, follow-through cap, same-candle tie behavior,
  SELL symmetry, and insufficient lookahead.
- Test the source-agnostic CSV loader with generated files and a representative
  M1 UTC bid CSV. Confirm it uses local files only and does not attempt network
  access.
- Test CST initialization, CHoCH, transition confirmation, old-trend resumption,
  strength labels, close-versus-wick configuration, and no future leakage.
- Test that local source comparisons report timestamp/close discrepancies,
  require explicit owner review, and block real-data scoring until the report is
  approved and its input hash still matches.
- Run the complete backtest over generated data and verify all six files load,
  alert CSV columns are correct, the validation split is chronological, and
  failed floors are reported without changing the rules.
- Add the eight owner-labeled fixtures from owner_labeled_fixtures.json to
  acceptance tests; expected_alert and expected_note must match exactly.
- Verify the M1 resampler emits only complete UTC M5/M15 bars and correctly
  aggregates OHLC.


5. ASSUMPTIONS TO REVIEW BEFORE IMPLEMENTATION CONTINUES

These details are not completely determined by OHLC candles or by the written
state-machine wording. The proposed defaults are explicit and should be
approved or corrected before they are finalized:

A. Same-candle target/adverse tie

OHLC bars do not reveal whether target or adverse price was reached first when
both occur in one candle. Proposed default: configurable same_candle_tie with
adverse_first as the conservative default. An optional target_first setting
allows an explicit alternative. The report and alert log should identify the
setting used.

B. Follow-through bonus after an adverse-first target

Proposed interpretation: once D_min is eventually reached, calculate additional
full D_min units from the maximum favorable excursion over all 12 future candles
and apply the capped follow-through bonus, even when the adverse level was
reached first. If that was not intended, adjust the rule before implementation.

C. CST transition confirmation

Proposed interpretation: after an uptrend CHoCH, wait for a newly confirmed
post-CHoCH swing low, then confirm downtrend on a break of that level; resume the
old uptrend if the saved pre-CHoCH swing-high level breaks first. Mirror this
logic for a downtrend CHoCH. If both wick-based break levels are crossed in the
same candle, keep CST in transition rather than choosing an order that OHLC
cannot prove.

D. Incomplete 12-candle windows

Proposed handling: leave the reward blank and mark INSUFFICIENT_LOOKAHEAD for an
alert without 12 subsequent candles. Such an alert still counts toward the
validation alert-count floor, but not toward the summed reward. It is reported
separately.

E. Calendar months

Proposed interpretation: align the 12-month synthetic sample to 12 consecutive
YYYY-MM calendar buckets, rather than exactly 365 elapsed days, so the 9/3 split
and 8-of-12 coverage check are unambiguous.

Some work began before the approval-first instruction. Current implementation:

- The local loader is source-agnostic and CSV-only; it returns UTC timestamp
  strings and validated Decimal OHLC records.
- The synthetic generator uses weekday-only candles over 12 calendar months with
  a fixed default seed.

- The backtest emits CST/strength, fixed-window reward scores, chronological
